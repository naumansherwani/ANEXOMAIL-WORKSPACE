# WHAT A WORLD-CLASS COMPANY ADMIN DESK CONTAINS, AND WHERE EACH PIECE GOES (his 22 Nov 2026 02:50 order)
Method: web research (below) + his own Desk rules. The CODE folders are founder-web/src/desk/modules/<group>/<key>/{meta.ts,README.md} with ONE list in modules/registry.ts (the rail reads it); this table is the research view and adds modules still to be registered (QA scoring, workforce capacity, customer-consented access, notifications, privacy requests). Nothing here is built unless the phase says LIVE; AD2 (staff IAM) is LIVE.
Layout decision (his word): CASES live in ANEXOMAIL WORKSPACE with Inboxes; the folder ADMIN DESK holds the per-user views (Managed Move-In, People, CRM); Company holds Team and Audit.

## Research (what the leaders ship, 22 Nov 2026)
* Front's own list of best-in-class help-desk features: omnichannel inbox, roles and permissions, real-time collaboration, automated routing, SLA tracking, templates, AI drafting/summaries/tagging, analytics (volume, response, resolution, CSAT), integrations/open API, shift scheduling and handoff, no-code workflows, multi-language. Source: https://front.com/blog/help-desk-system-features
* Zendesk Admin Center / Enterprise: roles, workflows, SLAs, analytics, audit: https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408832379546-Admin-Center-settings and https://www.zendesk.com/business/enterprise/support-enterprise-plan/
* Privileged-access patterns for staff viewing customer data (approval before access, access transparency log): Google Cloud https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/security/privileged-access-management (the Workspace Access Approval page itself could not be fetched, so module 15 is designed from the Cloud PAM pattern, not copied).
Where the Desk goes beyond them (his rules): device-bound passkeys only, hash-chained ledger, zero standing privilege, Rule 52 signed answers verified in the browser, no third-party vendor, AI drafting parked until July 2027 (his earlier word).
