**Rule:** koi jhoota inbox, jhoote contacts, jhoote graphs, jhoote "sample customers" nahi. Khali screen ki jagah **asli kaam ka agla qadam**.
## Pehle 10 minute (asli actions, checklist)
1. **Address claim** (asli @anexomail.com).
2. **Welcome email asli pipeline se** (system sender -> user ke asli mailbox mein, asli SMTP/IMAP) — ek kaam ke saath: "Reply to this".
3. **Apna doosra email jodo / forward** (instructions asli; Move-In pilot offer link).
4. **Pehla meeting / booking link** banao (asli).
5. **ANEXOChat**: ek sathi ko invite (asli link) ya khud ko note bhejo; message -> Task karke dekho.
6. **Pro jhalak**: Snooze, Schedule send — asli chalte hain, "Pro" chip ke saath.
Har qadam par saaf progress (5/6) — data khali hai to "yahan aap ka pehla ... aayega" + ek button.
